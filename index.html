Reel

Reel is a personal, self-hosted media player. It shows photos and videos from the developer's own Google Drive as a swipeable feed, served from a computer on the developer's home network.

Who it is for

Reel is used by a single person, its developer. It is not offered as a service and there is no public sign-up.

Google Drive access

Reel requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly) so it can list and play the signed-in user's own media files. It never creates, modifies, shares or deletes files.
